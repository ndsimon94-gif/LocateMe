A retreat center & community sanctuary · the foothills outside Lyons, Colorado

What the world needs more of.

Attention. Play. Community. Silence. Wildness. Creativity. Ritual. Movement. Wonder. Boredom. Time outside. Time together. Working with our hands. Learning from elders. Sitting around fires. Looking at stars. Doing things we're bad at. Knowing our neighbors. Being unreachable for a while.

Modern life has given us extraordinary things. Somewhere along the way we started losing others. Leela is a piece of land in the Colorado foothills where those things have room to come back.

Leela is a living place

An ordinary Wednesday.

Leela isn't a venue that sits empty until someone books a retreat. People live here, work here, and wander in. On any given day, this is roughly what you'd find.

Mondays and Tuesdays the land rests. There's no morning bell and fewer people around. This is what those days look like.

Times shift with the seasons, and more than a little with the weather.

The land

A house, a few yurts, a camp, and a lot of sky.

Leela started modestly and is growing slowly. Some of it is rustic. We think that's part of the point.

The House

The communal heart of Leela. Kitchen, library, a practice room, a fireplace, and a long table for meals. Tea is almost always on.

The Yurts

Simple, beautiful places to sleep, tucked into the trees. Good beds, warm light, a wood stove, and nothing on the walls that needs charging.

The Camp

Tent sites set out with care, a fire circle, and a view of the ridge. For people who'd rather sleep a little closer to the ground.

How Leela stays open

The deeper experiences pay for the simpler ones.

Retreats, stays, and workshops are how Leela pays its bills. They're also how it keeps morning practice free, how community dinners stay by donation, and how people get scholarships, residencies, and work-trade spots.

People who can afford the deeper paid experiences help make the simpler community ones available to everyone. We think that's a good arrangement.
