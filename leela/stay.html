Stay awhile

A few days at the speed of the place.

Sleep in a yurt or at the camp. Come for a retreat, a residency, or no reason at all. Most people say it takes about a day and a half to really arrive.

The communal heart

The House

Everyone passes through the House. It holds the kitchen, the dining table, the library, the practice room, and a big fireplace. People make tea here, read here, get bodywork here, and have the kind of conversations that go on too long.

The House isn't lodging. It's the living room for everyone staying on the land and for anyone who drops in during land hours.

Sleep here

The Yurts

Simple, beautiful rooms tucked into the landscape. Each has a real bed with good linens, warm lamps, a wood stove for cold nights, a small table, and a window that frames the ridge. They're comfortable without being fussy.

Bathrooms and showers are a short walk away. Bring slippers.

1–2 people (one yurt sleeps 4)

Sleep closer to the ground

The Camp

Campsites laid out with care: level tent pads, a shared fire circle, and room between neighbors. Guests at the camp share the House, the meals, and the mornings with everyone else.

Ways to stay

Some reasons to spend the night.

A few slow days

No program required. Come for morning practice, walk the land, read in the library, eat with whoever's around, and go to bed when it gets dark.

A retreat

Silent retreats, digital sabbaths, wilderness weekends, and workshops that run for several days. Lodging and meals are part of the retreat.

Upcoming retreats

A residency

Artists, writers, and makers can stay for two to six weeks in exchange for a small amount of teaching or making with the community. Some residencies are fully funded.

Applications open twice a year.

Work-trade

Trade twenty hours a week in the kitchen, garden, or on the trails for a place to sleep and a seat at the table. A good way to get to know Leela from the inside.

About work-trade

Growing slowly

Built a little at a time.

For now, Leela is a house, a handful of yurts, and a camp. That's deliberate. We'd rather build carefully, as the land and the money allow, than build fast.

Over the coming years we hope to add a few of these. When they arrive, you'll hear about it here first.

  • Cabins
  • A sauna and bathhouse
  • Art studios
  • A workshop
  • An outdoor kitchen
  • Meditation shelters
  • Gardens
  • More places to gather

What the world needs more of.

A retreat center and community sanctuary in the foothills outside Lyons, Colorado. Leela is a clear-minded, substance-free place.
