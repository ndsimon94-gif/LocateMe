The idea

One simple question.

What does the world need more of? Leela is an attempt to make physical space for the answers: a few acres of foothills where some of the things modern life is losing can grow back.

What we're losing

Somewhere along the way.

Modern life has given us extraordinary things. We don't want to give them back. But somewhere along the way, we started losing others, mostly without noticing, and mostly without choosing to.

Attention. Play. Community. Silence. Wildness. Creativity. Ritual. Movement. Wonder. Boredom. Time outside. Time together. Working with our hands. Learning from elders. Sitting around fires. Looking at stars. Doing things we're bad at. Knowing our neighbors. Being unreachable for a while.

Leela exists so these things have a place to flourish again. It's a place for remembering some of what it means to be human.

What kind of place this is

A little of everything, and none of them exactly.

  • A yoga retreat.
  • A spa.
  • A monastery.
  • A summer camp.
  • An artist colony.
  • A wilderness school.

Leela contains something of all of them. If you imagine a monastery, a summer camp, a wilderness school, an artist colony, and an old mountain lodge sharing a kitchen, you're getting close.

It's a retreat center, a contemplative sanctuary, a wild place, a creative community, and a gathering place for the town down the road. On different days it leans different ways.

Three movements

Back to yourself, each other, and the world.

Beneath all the programming are three simple movements. Leela lives where they overlap.

Come back to yourself

Attention, silence, rest, meditation, practice, embodiment, reflection, boredom, presence.

The morning sit, the empty bench, the afternoon with nothing scheduled. Time to notice what you notice.

Come back to each other

Community, play, meals, storytelling, celebration, service, elders, conversation, music, laughter.

The long table, the fire circle, the dish line after dinner. The places where strangers turn into neighbors.

Come back to the world

Nature, wildness, making, stewardship, seasons, animals, weather, wonder, working with the hands, stars.

The ridge, the garden, the woodpile, the first snow. The sky at night, which out here is still very much there.

Sacred and a little silly.

There should be warmth, and humor, and quiet, and every so often something slightly strange. Mostly, Leela should feel human.

Spirit

Open, curious, and respectful.

Leela is spiritually open and belongs to no single tradition. Buddhist practice and the dharma run deep here. You'll find a Buddha on the altar in the practice room, and Buddhist teachers lead many of our meditation retreats.

You'll also find sacred art from other traditions, folk art, sculpture, handmade objects, things found on the land, and work made by guests and resident artists. We try to honor each tradition as itself rather than blending them into one soft mood.

You can spend three days at Leela without believing anything in particular. Plenty of people do.

Technology & attention

Somewhere nothing is trying to get your attention.

Leela isn't against technology. Technology is useful, and we use it. But human attention deserves a few places where nothing is trying to capture it.

Many gatherings are phone-free. There's a basket by the door of the House, and a lot of people are surprised how glad they are to leave their phone in it. The practice room, the library, and the dinner table are screen-free. Some retreats include a full digital sabbath.

Signal on the land is patchy anyway. We've decided to count that as a feature.

How the place is made

Simple. Beautiful. Enough.

Leela started with limited resources, and some of it is rustic. We're not hiding that. Expect gravel paths, wood heat, and a fair amount of weather.

We spend money where it counts: good bedding, warm lighting, careful building, beautiful objects, clean spaces, good food, and things made by hand. Comfort without unnecessary luxury. We'd like Leela to feel intentional rather than expensive.

A clear-minded place

Leela is substance-free.

Leela is a clear-minded, substance-free property. We ask guests to leave alcohol and recreational drugs at home. It keeps the land quiet, the evenings honest, and the mornings easy.

Prescribed medications are, of course, your own business.
