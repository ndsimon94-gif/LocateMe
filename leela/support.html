Keep it open

Some things should be free.

Morning practice. A seat at the community dinner. An afternoon on the land. Leela pays for these the old-fashioned way: by also offering things worth paying for.

How it works

The deeper experiences pay for the simpler ones.

People who can afford retreats, stays, and workshops help make simpler community experiences available to everyone. That's the whole model.

About prices

Pay the price that fits.

We set prices so Leela can last, and we mean them. We also know that the people who might need a place like this most are often the ones least able to pay for it.

So most of our programs have more than one price. If you can pay the higher end of a sliding scale, you're helping someone else come. If you need the lower end, that's what it's there for. We don't ask questions.

Ways in, ways to help

Everybody contributes something.

Scholarships

Partial and full scholarships are available for every retreat. A short note about why you'd like to come is all we ask.

Work-trade

Trade time in the kitchen, garden, or on the trails for lodging, meals, or retreat tuition. Stays run from a weekend to a full season.

Volunteering

Join a Saturday land day, help cook a community dinner, or lend a skill. Carpenters, gardeners, and people who like washing dishes are especially welcome.

Teaching & elders

Know how to do something by hand, tell a good story, or remember how things used to be done? We'd love for you to teach it.

Giving

Gifts of any size go straight to community access: free programs, scholarships, and residencies. Some people give monthly. Some people give firewood.
