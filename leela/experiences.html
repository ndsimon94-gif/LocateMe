Experiences

Seven kinds of time.

Almost everything at Leela falls somewhere among these seven. They shape the calendar, but they also describe an ordinary afternoon here, when nothing is scheduled at all.

Come back to yourself

Presence

A place to stop consuming and start noticing.

Meditation · Dharma · Silence · Contemplation · Breath · Tea · Walking · Attention

Morning practice runs five days a week and is always free. There are dharma talks, silent retreats, walking meditation on the loop trail, and tea made slowly enough that the making counts.

  • Morning sit, Wednesday to Sunday
  • Silent weekend retreats
  • Monthly dharma talk
  • Tea practice on the porch

Come back to the world

Wildness

Remembering that humans are animals too.

Foraging · Wilderness skills · Backpacking · Nature awareness · Primitive skills · Solo time · Fasting · Ecology

The foothills are good teachers. Learn to read tracks, make fire, find water, name the plants underfoot, and sit alone in one spot long enough for the birds to forget about you.

  • Seasonal foraging walks
  • Wilderness skills courses
  • Guided overnight backpacking
  • Solo time on the land, with preparation and support

Come back to each other

Play

Adults need recess too.

Games · Dance · Improvisation · Exploration · Silliness · Costumes · Unstructured time

Not everything meaningful needs to be productive. Capture the flag at dusk. Improv in the House. A costume party for no reason. An afternoon with no plan that turns into something.

  • Game nights
  • Barn dances and ecstatic dance
  • Improv for the terrified
  • The occasional costume party

Come back to the world

Making

Remembering the intelligence of our hands.

Painting · Drawing · Ceramics · Woodworking · Natural crafts · Writing · Cooking · Gardening · Music

Process matters more than performance. Come to paint, whittle, write, throw a lopsided bowl, or cook for twenty. Nobody is grading.

  • Open making afternoons
  • Bad Drawing Night
  • Writing mornings
  • Resident artist workshops

Come back to each other

Connection

Places where strangers can become neighbors.

Shared meals · Storytelling · Conversation · Elders · Community work · Fires · Music · Celebration

The community dinner is the closest thing Leela has to a front door. Come once and you'll know a few names. Come a few times and you'll be the one explaining where the bowls go.

  • Thursday community dinner
  • Storytelling nights around the fire
  • Song circles
  • Seasonal feasts

Come back to yourself

Restoration

Sometimes the most useful thing we can do is nothing.

Yoga · Bodywork · Cold water · Hammocks · Sleep · Digital sabbaths · Slow mornings

Rest here isn't a reward for being productive. Take a gentle yoga class, book bodywork in the House, find a hammock, or sleep until you wake up on your own.

  • Gentle morning movement
  • Bodywork by appointment
  • Digital sabbath weekends
  • A sauna, someday soon

Come back to the world

Stewardship

Leave Leela a little better than you found it.

Gardening · Cooking · Trail work · Stacking wood · Ecological restoration · Building · Cleaning · Caring for shared spaces

Guests don't only consume Leela. Now and then they also help take care of it. Shared work is one of the best ways we know to arrive somewhere and to get to know people.

  • Saturday land days
  • Trail and restoration crews
  • Kitchen shifts
  • Work-trade stays

What we're protecting

Things worth making room for.

Modern culture has been quietly squeezing out some very old human experiences. Leela sets aside space for them on purpose.

Intergenerational life

Younger and older people spending real time together. Elders teach crafts, practical skills, cooking, music, natural knowledge, and what they've learned about living. Wisdom doesn't only belong to professional teachers.

Rites of passage

Thoughtful ways to mark the big turns in a life, with no particular religion required.

Coming of age · Marriage · Parenthood · Divorce · Grief · Retirement · Big birthdays · Transitions

Grief and mortality

Gentle, unhurried places for what we usually avoid talking about. Grief walks, remembrance fires, death cafés, memorial gatherings, and conversations about aging.

Boredom

Boredom isn't a problem we always need to solve. Some of Leela is set aside for nothing: no programming, no screens, no instructions, no optimizing. Just time.

Useful incompetence

Draw badly. Sing badly. Learn to whittle. Try juggling. Write a poem nobody will read. Make an ugly bowl. Adults need places to do things without being good at them.

Shared work

Work can be a practice too. Cook together, stack wood, tend the garden, repair the trail, wash the dishes. Showing up to help is part of being here.

Oral culture

Knowledge passed from person to person, out loud.

Storytelling · Poetry · Folk songs · Myths · Local history · Personal stories

Celebration

Leela is not a place for fixing people. It's a place for being alive. Feasts, dancing, costumes, solstice fires, harvests, birthdays, ridiculous games, and joy nobody planned.
