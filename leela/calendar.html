What's on · Autumn & early winter

Come for something.

Some of it happens every week. Some of it happens once a year when the light changes. All of it is open to people who have never been here before.

Every week

The heartbeat.

Wed–Sun · 7:00am

Morning practice

Forty minutes of sitting, a little movement, then tea. Free, always.

Wed–Sun · 10am–4pm

Land hours

Walk, read, sit, wander. The library and porch are open.

Thursdays · 6:30pm

Community dinner

One long table. By donation. Stay for dishes.

Saturdays · 9am–noon

Land day

Garden, trails, woodpile. Lunch for everyone who helps.

Sundays · 4pm

Open making

Tables, paper, clay, and tools. Bring a project or start one.

Mon & Tue

The land rests

No programs. A good day to come another day.

Hosting

Bring your own gathering.

Teachers, artists, and small groups sometimes host retreats and workshops at Leela. If what you do fits the spirit of the place, we'd love to hear about it.

Tell us what you have in mind, who it's for, and what time of year you're thinking. Write to hosting@leela.place.

What the world needs more of.

A retreat center and community sanctuary in the foothills outside Lyons, Colorado. Leela is a clear-minded, substance-free place.
